## Talking points per step

- **Why a receivable and not a loan.** The buyer's own EIP-712 signature, naming our router as payee, is stronger evidence than anything we issue. Nothing is financeable without it.
- **Why the router is modified.** One added opcode, `ReceivableCredit` on `Opcode._21`, makes every fill read the receivable's on-chain state, the lender's seller rule and the lender's per-buyer cap. The runtime is 23,759 B because `XYCConcentrateSwap` was dropped to fit EIP-170.
- **Shared liquidity.** No pool. The lender's USDC stays in the lender's wallet and backs both strategies. Base yield is measured from real swaps by a scripted trader and is labelled "scripted volume".
- **Why a 2-of-2 spend wallet.** x402 on Hedera debits the client's own account, so a contract cannot pay. The co-signer refuses any transfer that does not match an open reservation, and the facilitator refuses a payment without both signatures (proven in S3).
