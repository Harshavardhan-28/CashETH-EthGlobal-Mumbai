# Market feedback

How we collect feedback, and every response we have received. Only real responses from people outside the team go in the log; nothing here is paraphrased into something it was not.

## How to give feedback

Open a [feedback issue](https://github.com/Harshavardhan-28/CashETH-EthGlobal-Mumbai/issues/new?template=feedback.yml) after trying the Claude Code seller ([CLAUDE_CODE_DEMO.md](CLAUDE_CODE_DEMO.md)), `pnpm demo` ([DEMO.md](DEMO.md)), or just reading the docs. The form takes about five minutes.

## Who we are asking

| Group | What we want to learn |
| --- | --- |
| AI agent developers | Is raising credit from an agent understandable, and are budgets and co-signing acceptable controls? |
| x402 API sellers | Would credit-funded buyers matter to them? |
| DeFi lenders and Aqua LPs | What APR, advance rate, tenor and buyer reputation would they lend at? |
| Invoice finance professionals | Is a buyer-signed confirmation strong enough evidence, and what is missing? |

## Feedback cycle

1. Someone tries the flow and files the form.
2. We tag each response with the change it suggests and link the issue or commit that acts on it.
3. We follow up with anyone who leaves contact details once the change ships, and record whether it fixed their problem.
